Connect your music (Last.fm)

Link Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music to a free Last.fm account, then create a free API key at last.fm/api/account/create. Each place will show what you really listened to there.

Your days stay in your browser. Only what you tick as public or shared is sent, and points near your private places are removed first. Keep the page open while recording: mobile browsers stop the GPS when the screen locks. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
