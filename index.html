Connect your music (Last.fm)

Link Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music to a free Last.fm account, then create a free API key at last.fm/api/account/create. Each place will show what you really listened to there.

Your location and settings stay in your browser. Keep the page open while recording: mobile browsers stop the GPS when the screen locks. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors © CARTO.
